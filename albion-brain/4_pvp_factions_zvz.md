# Albion Online: PvP, Factions & ZvZ Warfare

## PvP Mechanics

### Flagging System
- **Unflagged**: Can only PvP in PvP zones
- **Flagged**: Can PvP in Blue zones (risky)
- **Consequences**: Losing unflagged = lose gear
- **Risk/Reward**: Better silver gain when flagged

### Combat Damage Types
- **Physical**: Swords, axes, ranged weapons
- **Holy**: Healing, protection, area denial
- **Nature**: Buffs, crowd control, healing
- **Arcane**: Crowd control, vulnerability, burst
- **Fire/Frost**: Damage over time, control

### Crowd Control Effects
- **Stun**: Disable for 2 sec, break with damage
- **Snare**: Slow movement 50% for 5 sec
- **Root**: 0% movement, can't escape
- **Silence**: Can't use abilities, ~3 sec
- **Knockback**: Force away from position

## Hellgates (Competitive PvP)

### 2v2 Hellgates (Solo Queue)
- **Arena**: Enclosed map, 2 players vs 2 players
- **Duration**: 10-15 minutes
- **Ranking**: Ladder-based, rewards for wins
- **Loot**: Minimal, focus on rank
- **Skill Floor**: Medium, meta builds required

### 3v3 Hellgates
- **Arena**: Larger map, 3v3 teams
- **Coordination**: Needed for tactics
- **Difficulty**: Medium-high
- **Reward**: Better rewards than 2v2

### 5v5 Hellgates
- **Team Sport**: Full 5-player battles
- **Strategy**: Tank/DPS/Healer composition
- **Tactics**: Focus target, split damage, control map
- **Competitive**: Ranked ladder with prizes

### Meta Hellgate Builds (T6)
- **Tank**: Mace/Shield + Plate armor (absorb damage)
- **DPS**: Sword + Spear (high damage, mobility)
- **Healer**: Holy Staff + Nature Robe (sustain)
- **Counter**: Fire Staff + Frost Focus (zone control)

## Faction Warfare

### Faction System
- **Red Army**: vs. Blue Sentinels
- **Outpost Wars**: Battle for territory every 2 hours
- **Engagement**: Open PvP in faction zone
- **Rewards**: Faction points, special rewards
- **Ranking**: Leaderboard for kills/assists

### Outpost Battles
- **Objectives**: Capture flag or hold point
- **Teams**: Auto-joined by faction
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Loot**: Faction points + rewards
- **Entry**: Caerleon faction portal

### Faction Gear & Rewards
- **Faction Points**: Earn for kills/objectives
- **Redeem**: Special faction armor/weapons
- **Badges**: Display faction rank

## ZvZ (Large-Scale Guild Warfare)

### Territory Control
- **Black Zone Territories**: 50+ guild cities
- **Ownership**: Guilds own and tax
- **Tax Income**: Generate passive silver
- **Territory Wars**: Weekly defense battles

### Territory War Mechanics
- **Attacking Guild**: Schedule attack time
- **Defending Guild**: Prepare defenses
- **Battle Format**: 20v20 to 40v40 players
- **Objective**: Destroy enemy gates/towers
- **Victory**: First team to break through wins

### ZvZ Coordination
- **Guild Banking**: Pooled resources
- **Callouts**: Voice comms for tactics
- **Fleet Composition**: Balance of tank/DPS/heal
- **Supply Lines**: Bring potions, food, extra gear
- **Tactics**: Frontal assault, flanks, sieges

### ZvZ Combat Roles
| Role | Weapon | Armor | Purpose |
|------|--------|-------|---------|
| Tank | Mace/Shield | Plate | Hold front line |
| Damage | Sword/Bow | Leather | Kill priority targets |
| Healer | Holy Staff | Cloth | Keep team alive |
| Crowd Control | Fire/Frost | Mixed | Disable enemies |
| Ranged DPS | Crossbow | Leather | Back-line pressure |

## Anti-Gank Strategies

### Traveling Safely
- **Group Movement**: Never travel alone
- **Timing**: Avoid peak hours when gankers active
- **Route Variation**: Don't use same path twice
- **Communication**: Call out danger zones

### Build for Survival
- **Medium Armor**: Balance speed + defense
- **Mobility Weapon**: Spear (dash ability)
- **Escape Potion**: Keep mana potion ready
- **Defensive Buff**: Holy shield or armor buff

## Guild PvP Progression

1. **New Guild**: Solo ganking for silver
2. **Small Guild**: 5v5 Hellgate team
3. **Medium Guild**: Territory wars, 20v20
4. **Large Guild**: ZvZ, 40+ player battles
5. **Elite Guild**: Alliance warfare, politics
