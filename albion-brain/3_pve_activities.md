# Albion Online: PvE Activities

## Dungeons

### Solo Dungeons (Green Dungeons)
- **Solo Play**: 1 player, safe farming
- **Difficulty**: T4-T5
- **Duration**: 15-30 minutes
- **Mobs**: Solo-able, ~20-50 per run
- **Loot**: Common drops, chest rewards
- **Strategy**: Farm for gear/silver
- **Reward**: ~50k-100k silver per run

### Group Dungeons (Blue Dungeons)
- **5v5 PvE**: Requires 5 players
- **Difficulty**: T5-T6
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Bosses**: Mini-bosses with mechanics
- **Loot**: Shared table, everyone gets drops
- **Strategy**: Tank holds aggro, DPS stack damage, Healer keeps alive
- **Reward**: ~200k-500k silver split by group

### Corrupted Dungeons (PvP/PvE Hybrid)
- **1v1 or 2v2**: Instanced PvP + mobs
- **Difficulty**: T5-T6
- **Mobs Spawn**: During fight, random locations
- **Winner**: Defeats opponent + clears mobs
- **Loot**: Winner-take-all
- **Risk**: Lose gear if you lose

### Expeditions (Seasonal Events)
- **Group Content**: 5 players
- **Difficulty**: T5-T6
- **Dungeon Map**: Open layout, multiple bosses
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Mechanics**: Defeat bosses in order, avoid hazards
- **Reward**: Premium chest with rare drops

## Open World PvE

### World Bosses
- **Location**: Black/Red zones
- **Spawn Time**: Random, every 2-4 hours
- **Difficulty**: T6-T7
- **Players Needed**: 5-10 coordinated
- **Loot**: Rare drops, T7 gear possible
- **Risk**: Ganking common while farming boss

### Elite Mobs
- **Location**: Red/Black zones
- **Spawn**: Rare roaming packs
- **Difficulty**: T6-T7
- **Loot**: Premium materials
- **Strategy**: Kite and damage, avoid melee range

### Trash Farming
- **Activity**: Kill random mobs in zone
- **Difficulty**: T4-T7 depending on zone
- **Reward**: Slow but steady silver (100k/hour)
- **Risk**: Gankers, zone competition

## PvE Strategy

### Mob Weaknesses
- **Physical Mobs**: Use high armor + healing
- **Magic Mobs**: Dodge, use mana shield
- **Range Mobs**: Close distance, stun

### Gear for PvE
- **Tank**: Plate armor + mace/shield (high armor)
- **DPS**: Weapon based (swords/bows), medium armor
- **Healer**: Holy staff + cloth armor

### Income Comparison
| Activity | Duration | Reward | Risk |
|----------|----------|--------|------|
| Solo Dungeon | 25 min | 75k | Low |
| 5v5 Dungeon | 40 min | 250k | Low |
| World Boss | Variable | 500k+ | High |
| Trash Farm | 1 hour | 100k | Medium |
| Expedition | 25 min | 200k | Low |

## Leveling Path
1. Solo dungeons (T4) → Levels 1-20
2. Group dungeons (T5) → Levels 20-50
3. Expeditions (T5-T6) → Levels 50-80
4. Black zone content (T7) → Levels 80+

## Beginner PvE Rotation
- Farm solo dungeons for 3 days (silver + gear)
- Join group for 5v5 dungeons (teamwork)
- Try expeditions (learn mechanics)
- Progress to Black zones (better rewards)
