# Albion Online: Crafting & Refining

## Crafting System Basics

### Core Mechanics
- **Recipe Learning**: Buy from NPCs or learn from guilds
- **Crafting Power**: Increases speed + quality (passive skill)
- **Return Rate**: 80-100% of materials returned (80% at low skill)
- **Tax**: 5-10% of item value when crafting
- **Success Rate**: Increases with skill, never fails

### Crafting Professions
- **Weapons**: Swords, axes, bows, staves
- **Armor**: Helmets, chests, legs, shoes, gloves
- **Gathering**: Ore, wood, leather, fiber
- **Refining**: Convert raw → refined materials
- **Cooking**: Food, potions, buff consumables

### Skill Progression
- **Beginner**: 0-100 (low return %, slow speed)
- **Intermediate**: 100-200 (+5% return, +20% speed)
- **Advanced**: 200-300 (+10% return, +40% speed)
- **Expert**: 300+ (+20% return, 2x speed, can enchant)

## Refining Profits

### Ore Refining Example
- **T5 Ore**: Buy 100 @ 15k = 1500k
- **Refining**: 100 → 10 T5 Ingots (10:1 ratio)
- **Ingot Value**: 10 @ 20k = 200k
- **Tax**: 15k (10% on material value)
- **Profit**: 200k - 1500k - 15k = **Loss** (-1315k)

### WAIT: Reverse Flipping!
- **Better Play**: Buy cheap T5 Ingots (19k)
- **Reverse Refine**: Deconstruct into ore
- **Sell Ore**: T5 Ore @ 16k
- **Profit**: Arbitrage on price differences

## Enchantment Crafting

### Enchantment Levels
- **@1**: 10% stat boost, 1 Essence + base item
- **@2**: 20% stat boost, 2 Essences + @1 item
- **@3**: 30% stat boost, 4 Essences + @2 item

### Essence Requirements
- **T4 Enchant**: 10 Essences @ 5k = 50k
- **T5 Enchant**: 50 Essences @ 20k = 1000k
- **T6 Enchant**: 200 Essences @ 100k = 20000k

### Profitable Enchant Strategy
1. Buy underpriced base items (T5 swords cheap?)
2. Craft @1 enchantment (low cost)
3. Sell at markup (buyers want ready-made items)
4. **Profit**: 30-50% if prices align

## Transmutation (Advanced)

### Ancient Artifacts
- **Recipe**: Rare drop or high crafting level
- **Components**: Mix of old items + essences
- **Output**: Powerful unique items
- **Market**: Limited supply, high price (200k-2m)

## Crafting Tax Optimization

### Tax Calculation
- **Tax = Item Value × 0.05-0.10**
- **Higher Tier = Higher Tax**
- **Solution**: Craft in guild hall (often has tax reduction)

### Guild Benefits
- **Guild Hall Tax**: 2-5% vs. NPC 10%
- **Shared Resources**: Pool materials with guildmates
- **Bulk Crafting**: Lower per-item cost

## Gathering & Refining Loop

### Passive Income Loop
1. **Gather**: Farm ore in safe zones (1 hour)
2. **Refine**: Convert ore → ingots (30 min)
3. **Craft**: Make swords from ingots (30 min)
4. **Sell**: List on auction house
5. **Repeat**: Daily for 1m+ passive income

### Material Farming Hotspots
- **Fort Sterling**: Ore-rich, competitive
- **Lymhurst**: Wood-rich, safe
- **Bridgewatch**: Mixed, good profit
- **Thetford**: Stone (less valuable)
- **Martlock**: Leather, leather armor crafting
