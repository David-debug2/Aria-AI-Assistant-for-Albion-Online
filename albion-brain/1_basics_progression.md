# Albion Online: Basics & Progression

## Game Progression System

### Tier System (T1-T8)
- **T1-T4**: Starter content, readily available
- **T5-T6**: Mid-game, requires dungeons/crafting
- **T7-T8**: Endgame, rare drops, crafted from rare materials
- Each tier roughly 2-3x stronger than previous
- Enchantment levels: @0 (base) to @3 (max enhancement)

### Character Progression
1. **Starter Quest** → T4 gear reward
2. **Leveling Path**:
   - Solo dungeons (T4-T5)
   - 5v5 dungeons (T5-T6)
   - Expeditions (T5-T6, group content)
   - Hellgates (competitive, T6-T7)
   - Open-world content (T5-T8)

### Gear Enchantments
- **@1**: 10% stat boost (cheap to craft)
- **@2**: 20% stat boost (requires rare materials)
- **@3**: 30% stat boost (expensive, for endgame)

## Armor & Weapon Types

### Weapon Trees (18 types)
- **Melee**: Swords, Spears, Polearms, Axes, Hammers, Maces
- **Ranged**: Bows, Crossbows, Nature Staff
- **Magic**: Arcane, Fire, Holy, Dark, Frost Staffs
- **Support**: Healing Focus, Nature Focus

### Armor Slots
- Head: Helm (physical defense)
- Body: Chest (main defense + special effect)
- Legs: Leggings (dodge/defense)
- Feet: Shoes (movement speed)
- Hand: Gloves (attack speed or defense)

### Armor Types
- **Plate**: Heavy, high defense, slow movement
- **Leather**: Medium, balanced
- **Cloth**: Light, low defense, high magic damage

## Resource System

### Crafting Materials
- **Ore** → Ingots → Weapons/Armor
- **Wood** → Planks → Bows, Staves
- **Leather** → Armor pieces
- **Herbs** → Potions, food buffs
- **Soul Essences** → Enchantment materials

### Food & Potions
- **Food**: +Damage, +Defense, +Healing (30 min duration)
- **Potions**: Health restore, Mana restore (combat use)
- **Fish**: Premium food, +2 levels of stats

## Silver Economy
- Currency for trading, crafting taxes, NPC purchases
- Earned from: Dungeons, selling items, PvE mobs
- Crafting Tax: 5-10% of item value when crafting

## Newbie Recommendations

**Week 1**: T4 gear → Solo dungeons
**Week 2**: Level to 30 → Join guild, learn 5v5 dungeons
**Week 3**: Save silver for T5 gear → Expeditions
**Goal**: Reach T6 within 2 months = stable income

## Progression Bottleneck
- **T5 → T6**: Requires dungeon runs or island farming (slow)
- **Solution**: Join active guild for group content
- **Alternative**: Focus on crafting/refining for passive income
