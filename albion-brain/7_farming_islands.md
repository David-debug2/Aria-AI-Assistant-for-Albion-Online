# Albion Online: Farming & Island Management

## Island System

### Personal Island Ownership
- **Cost**: 50k silver (can be rented long-term)
- **Size**: Small/Medium/Large (determines crops)
- **Upgrades**: Better soil = better yields
- **Passive Income**: Farm runs itself, harvest weekly

### Island Farming Mechanics
- **Crop Types**: Cabbage, Carrots, Pumpkins, Wheat, Barley
- **Growth Time**: 7 days per cycle
- **Harvest**: Automatic after 7 days, can delay
- **Yield**: 5-15 units per plot depending on crop quality
- **Seasons**: Some crops only grow in certain seasons

### Farming Plots
- **Small Island**: 4 plots
- **Medium Island**: 8 plots
- **Large Island**: 12 plots

### Crop Values & Yields
| Crop | Grows | Yield | Value | Income |
|------|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| Cabbage | Spring | 10/plot | 5k | 50k |
| Carrot | Summer | 8/plot | 8k | 64k |
| Pumpkin | Fall | 12/plot | 3k | 36k |
| Wheat | Winter | 6/plot | 6k | 36k |
| Barley | Year-round | 5/plot | 7k | 35k |

### Medium Island Income (8 plots)
- **All Carrots**: 8 plots × 64k = 512k per week
- **Mixed Crops**: Average 400k per week
- **Passive**: No playing required after planting

### Island Upgrades
- **Fertile Soil**: +30% yield, costs 100k silver per plot
- **Premium Soil**: +50% yield, costs 500k per plot
- **Result**: 512k → 665k → 768k per week

## Mists Farming

### Mists Entry Requirements
- **Cost**: 5k silver per entry
- **Timer**: 24-hour cooldown per entry
- **Safe Zone**: No PvP, can't be ganked
- **Duration**: 30-60 minutes per run

### Mists Resources
- **Herbs**: Cabbage, Carrot, Pumpkin (all tiers)
- **Dense Deposits**: 2-3x normal yield
- **Rare Drops**: Soulcrafting materials, essences

### Mists Farming Strategy
1. **Enter**: Pay 5k, get teleported to Mists
2. **Gather**: Farm herbs from dense deposits (20-30 min)
3. **Exit**: Teleport out before leaving zone
4. **Profit**: 50k-150k per run depending on luck

## Open World Farming

### Red Zone Farming
- **Risk**: Gankers, competitive zones
- **Reward**: +50% resource yield vs. safe zones
- **Solo Strategy**: Farm off-peak hours
- **Group Strategy**: Bring guildmates for protection

### Best Farming Routes
- **Fort Sterling** (Wood): Minimal ganking, high density
- **Lymhurst** (Herbs): South of city, moderately safe
- **Bridgewatch** (Ore): Competitive, need group

### Farming Rotation
- **Morning** (Low activity): Red zones alone
- **Mid-day**: Group farming in Black zones
- **Evening** (Peak time): Mists farming (safe)

## Cooking & Food Production

### Cooking System
- **Recipe Learning**: Recipes from NPC or guilds
- **Ingredients**: Crops + meat/fish
- **Output**: Food buffs (+Damage, +Defense, +Healing)
- **Value**: Premium food sells well (high markup)

### Food Profit Opportunities
- **Simple Food**: Bread (wheat + time) = 1k → 3k (200%)
- **Premium Food**: Meat Pies (meat + crops) = 5k → 15k (200%)
- **Fish Stew**: Fish + herbs = 10k → 30k (200%)

### Food Market Spikes
- **Pre-War Days**: Demand +300%
- **Territory Resets**: +200% demand
- **New Player Influx**: +150% demand

## Fishing

### Fishing System
- **Mechanic**: Cast line, wait for bite, click to reel
- **Time per Fish**: 2-5 minutes
- **Yield**: 1-5 fish per cast
- **Value**: 1k-50k per fish depending on rarity

### Premium Fish
- **Mackerel**: 1k each (common)
- **Herring**: 5k each (uncommon)
- **Salmon**: 20k each (rare)
- **Legendary Fish**: 100k+ (ultra-rare, trophy)

### Fishing Locations
- **Safe Zones**: Always available, low yield
- **Red Zones**: Better fish, competitive
- **Black Zones**: Rare fish, dangerous

## New Player Farming Strategy

1. **Week 1**: Buy personal island (50k)
2. **Week 2-3**: Plant Carrots, earn 400k passively
3. **Week 4**: Upgrade soil, earn 600k+
4. **Week 5+**: Add Mists farming for active income
5. **Goal**: 1m+ weekly passive income by week 6
