# Albion Online: World Map & Zones

## Safe Zones (Blue Zones)
- **Starting Area**: Cities and towns, NPCs, traders
- **Key Cities**: Caerleon (main hub), Bridgewatch, Fort Sterling, Martlock, Lymhurst, Thetford
- **Features**: Auction House, Guild halls, Banks, Crafting stations
- **No PvP**: Completely safe, teleportable

## Contested Zones (Red/Yellow Zones)
- **Red Zones**: PvP enabled, 30+ players die/lose items
- **Yellow Zones**: Limited PvP, can't loot, death penalties
- **Risk/Reward**: Better resources, more dangerous
- **Danger Level**: T5+ resources available

## Black Zones (Black Zones)
- **Full Loot**: Dying costs you everything (armor, weapons, backpack)
- **PvP 24/7**: No safe zones, always dangerous
- **Premium Resources**: T7-T8 materials, rare drops
- **Guild Territory**: Guilds claim regions, own cities
- **For**: Hardcores, organized groups, raiders

## The Mists
- **Isolated Zone**: Can't be ganked while gathering
- **Solo Activity**: One player instance
- **Resource Gathering**: Farm without fear of PvP
- **Cooldown**: 24-hour wait between entry
- **Best For**: Solo farmers, avoiding ganks

## Hellgates (Portals)
- **Competitive PvP**: 2v2, 3v3, 5v5 instanced battles
- **Ranked Matches**: Rewards based on win rate
- **Entry Point**: From Black zones or Caerleon
- **Loot**: Minimal, focus on rewards/ranking
- **Risk**: Lose gear if you die (same as Black zone)

## Zone Difficulty Tiers
| Zone Type | PvP | Resources | Danger | Recommended Tier |
|-----------|-----|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Safe Zone | No | T1-T4 | Low | New players |
| Yellow | Yes | T3-T5 | Medium | T5+ players |
| Red | Yes | T4-T6 | High | T6+ groups |
| Black | Yes | T6-T8 | Critical | T7+ organized |
| Mists | No | T4-T6 | Low | Soloers |

## Zone Resources by Location
- **Bridgewatch**: Ore deposits, North territory
- **Fort Sterling**: Wood forests, East territory
- **Lymhurst**: Herbs/Fiber, South territory
- **Thetford**: Stone, West territory
- **Martlock**: Mixed resources, Central territory

## Travel & Portal Network
- **Teleportable**: Between Safe zones (5-15 min travel)
- **Manual Travel**: Across zone borders (risk of ganks)
- **Dungeon Portals**: Instanced content entry points
- **Portal Cooldown**: 30 seconds between travels

## Zone Rotation (Political)
- Guilds fight for Black zone territory control
- Territories grant: Tax income, resource nodes, city bonuses
- Changes weekly based on alliance wars
