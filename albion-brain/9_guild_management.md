# Albion Online: Guild Management & Territory Control

## Guild System Basics

### Creating a Guild
- **Founder Fee**: 5k silver
- **Initial Members**: 1 (founder)
- **Max Members**: 200 (upgradeable to 500)
- **Guild Hall**: Unlocked at 10 members
- **Taxes**: Set by leader (0-10%)

### Guild Roles & Permissions
- **Guild Master**: Full control
- **Officers**: Manage members, events, treasury
- **Captains**: Lead squads, organize activities
- **Members**: Play, contribute resources
- **Recruit**: Probationary members (7 days)

### Guild Ranks (By Activity)
| Rank | Members | Territory | Income |
|------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Solo | 1 | None | Low |
| Small | 10-20 | Outposts only | Medium |
| Medium | 50-100 | 1-3 territories | High |
| Large | 150-300 | 5+ territories | Very High |
| Mega | 400+ | 10+ territories | Massive |

## Territory Control

### Territory Warfare Overview
- **Total Territories**: ~50 across map
- **Ownership**: Individual guilds own and tax
- **Tax Income**: Guilds generate silver from territory
- **Defense**: Weekly scheduled wars

### Territory Types

**Residential Territory**
- Income: 50k-100k per week
- Defense: Moderate (small wars)
- Tax: 5% on items traded
- Best For: Small/medium guilds

**Industrial Territory**
- Income: 100k-200k per week
- Resources: Ore/wood/leather deposits
- Defense: High (contested often)
- Best For: Crafting guilds

**Commercial Territory**
- Income: 150k-300k per week
- Markets: Trading hubs
- Traffic: Lots of players = more activity
- Best For: Large trading guilds

### Territory War Mechanics

**Declaring War**
1. Guild pays 50k silver
2. Choose attack time (24-48h notice)
3. Schedule battle window (15-30 min)
4. Enemy guild gets notification

**Defense Setup**
- **Gates**: Build defensive structures
- **Towers**: Anti-siege towers (deal damage)
- **Traps**: Delayed damage zones
- **Garrison**: Station defenders nearby

**Battle Format**
- **Attacker Goal**: Destroy enemy gates/core
- **Defender Goal**: Kill attackers, hold position
- **Team Size**: 20v20 to 40v40 (depends on territory)
- **Duration**: 20-30 minutes
- **Victory**: First side to achieve objective wins

### ZvZ (Large-Scale Combat)

**Squad Composition (5v5 unit)**
- Tank (Mace/Plate)
- 2x Damage (Sword/Bow)
- Healer (Holy)
- Crowd Control (Frost/Arcane)

**Fleet Composition (25v25)**
- 3-4 Tank squads (hold line)
- 3-4 DPS squads (damage enemy)
- 1-2 Healer squads (support)
- 1 CC squad (disable enemies)

**ZvZ Tactics**
1. **Initial Engage**: Tanks advance, draw enemy fire
2. **Flank**: DPS squads hit from sides
3. **Heal Focus**: Healers keep tanks alive
4. **Retreat**: Regroup if losing numbers

## Guild Treasury Management

### Income Sources
| Source | Weekly | Monthly |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Territory Tax | 50-300k | 200k-1.2m |
| Guild Market Tax | 20-100k | 80-400k |
| Member Deposits | 0-200k | 0-800k |
| Bounties/Events | 0-50k | 0-200k |
| **Total** | **70-650k** | **280-2.6m** |

### Treasury Spending
- **Defense Build**: 500k per territory
- **Member Support**: Loans, gear help
- **War Preparation**: Stock potions, food
- **Guild Halls**: Upgrades, crafting stations

### Smart Treasury Use
1. **Reserve Fund**: Keep 6 months expenses
2. **War Fund**: For territory defense
3. **Member Bonus**: Distribute excess income
4. **Expansion**: Invest in guild upgrades

## Guild Progression

### Timeline to Territory Control
- **Week 1**: Form guild, recruit 10+ members
- **Week 2-3**: Establish guild hall, start activities
- **Week 4-6**: Prepare for first territory attempt
- **Week 7**: Claim first residential territory
- **Month 3**: Upgrade to industrial territory
- **Month 6**: Control 3-5 territories = established guild

### Territory Warfare Preparation

**Before War**
- Recruit 20-40 active players
- Stock potions/food for 2 hours
- Plan squad compositions
- Scout enemy defenses

**Day of War**
- Gather in staging area
- Assign squads and roles
- Call out enemies via voice comms
- Adapt tactics based on enemy moves

## Diplomacy & Alliances

### Alliance System
- **Purpose**: Coordinate between guilds
- **Benefits**: Share territories, support wars
- **Members**: 3-5 guilds typically
- **Risk**: Internal conflicts, betrayal

### Conflict Resolution
- **Boundaries**: Define territory ownership
- **Respect**: Don't poach members
- **Support**: Help in defensive wars
- **Trust**: Build reputation over time

## Common Guild Issues & Solutions

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Low activity | Poor leader | Recruit active officers |
| Broke treasury | Over-spending | Budget wars, tax increases |
| Territory loss | Weak defense | Train guild in combat |
| Member drama | Poor management | Clear rules, fair leadership |
| No progression | Lazy planning | Set weekly goals |

## Guild Recommendations

**Small Guild** (10-30 members)
- Focus: PvE dungeons, crafting
- Goal: Learn game, build community
- Territory: Skip for now, too risky

**Medium Guild** (50-100 members)
- Focus: Territory warfare, endgame content
- Goal: Control 1-2 territories
- Income: 100-200k weekly from tax

**Large Guild** (200+ members)
- Focus: Dominate region, politics
- Goal: Alliance wars, multiple territories
- Income: 500k-1m+ weekly

## Leadership Tips

1. **Recruit Quality**: Members > numbers
2. **Communicate**: Weekly goals, updates
3. **Organize Events**: Regular wars, dungeons
4. **Be Fair**: Rules apply equally
5. **Invest in Community**: Fun > profit
6. **Adapt**: Meta changes, be flexible
7. **Support Members**: Help new players
8. **Build Culture**: Identity matters
