# Albion Online: Weapons & Armor

## Weapon Trees Overview

### Melee Weapons

#### Swords (Primary DPS)
- **T1-T3**: Learning weapon, low damage
- **T4-T5**: Standard DPS, mid-range (3m)
- **T6-T7**: High damage, versatile
- **T8**: Rare legendary, max damage
- **Playstyle**: Mobile, gap-closer abilities
- **Example Build**: Claymore (@2) + Leather + Holy
- **Market Price**: T5 = 1500k, T6 = 5000k

#### Axes (Burst Damage)
- **Speciality**: High single-target damage, execute ability
- **T5-T6**: Common in hellgates
- **Risk**: Slower attack speed, melee only
- **Counter**: Ranged weapons, kiting

#### Spears (Crowd Control)
- **Dash Ability**: Quick reposition, escape
- **Speciality**: CC chains, group control
- **Popular**: Open world PvP, flagging
- **Advantage**: Mobility over damage

#### Maces (Tank Weapon)
- **Damage Type**: Physical + armor penetration
- **Tank Combo**: Mace + Plate armor + Healing spell
- **Ability**: Stun enemies, disable enemies
- **T5 Price**: 800k

#### Hammers (AOE Damage)
- **Speciality**: Large area attacks, group damage
- **Ability**: Knock enemies back, AoE slam
- **ZvZ Use**: Excellent for large battles
- **T5-T6**: Popular in wars

#### Polearms (Range Melee)
- **Range**: 4m (longer than swords)
- **Speciality**: Poke enemies, kite while dealing damage
- **T5-T6**: Good beginner weapon
- **Price**: T5 = 1200k

### Ranged Weapons

#### Bows (Sustained DPS)
- **Playstyle**: Medium range (10m+), sustained damage
- **Attack Speed**: Fast, constant pressure
- **Speciality**: Damage output over time
- **T5 Cost**: 1800k
- **Popular**: Dungeons, trash farming

#### Crossbows (Burst Ranged)
- **Playstyle**: High damage, slower attacks
- **Ability**: Power shot, piercing shots
- **T5-T6**: Popular in hellgates
- **Price**: T5 = 2000k

### Magic Staffs

#### Holy Staff (Healing/Support)
- **Primary Use**: Group healing
- **Playstyle**: Back-line support, healing focus
- **Ability**: Large heal, emergency heal + damage buff
- **Tank Combo**: Holy staff = essential for tank survival
- **T5 Price**: 1500k

#### Fire Staff (Damage/Control)
- **Playstyle**: AOE burst damage
- **Ability**: Fireball (AoE), immobilize
- **ZvZ Use**: Crowd control in large battles
- **Speciality**: Zone denial

#### Nature Staff (Healing + Buffs)
- **Playstyle**: Healing + damage buff support
- **Ability**: Group heal + damage increase
- **Synergy**: Nature + Holy = ultimate healer combo
- **T5 Price**: 1600k

#### Arcane/Frost/Dark Staffs
- **Speciality**: Control (freeze, stun, root)
- **Playstyle**: Crowd control > damage
- **Ability**: Unique CC chains per staff
- **T5 Price**: 1400-1600k

## Armor Breakdown

### Armor Classes & Stats

#### Plate Armor (Tank)
- **Defense**: +40% physical damage reduction
- **Movement**: -20% movement speed (heavy)
- **Health**: +50% HP pool
- **Use**: Tanks, melee fighters
- **Example**: Knight Plate Helm + Chest (@2)
- **T5 Price**: 2000k for full set

#### Leather Armor (Balanced)
- **Defense**: +20% damage reduction
- **Movement**: Normal movement speed
- **Damage**: +10% DPS
- **Use**: DPS players, versatile
- **Example**: Dragonhide Armor set
- **T5 Price**: 1500k full set

#### Cloth Armor (Caster)
- **Defense**: +10% reduction (low)
- **Movement**: +10% movement speed (light)
- **Magic**: +30% magic damage
- **Use**: Mages, damage dealers
- **Example**: Assassin Jacket (cloth)
- **T5 Price**: 1200k full set

## Enchantment Impact

### Stat Boost by Enchantment
| Level | Boost | Cost | Cumulative |
|-------|-------|------|-----------|
| @0 | +0% | - | 100% |
| @1 | +10% | 50k | 110% |
| @2 | +20% | 500k | 120% |
| @3 | +30% | 5000k | 130% |

### Enchantment Strategy
- **@1**: Always worth for expensive items (1% cost)
- **@2**: Worth for T6+ gear (10% cost)
- **@3**: Only for elite endgame gear (25% cost)

**Sword/Holy/Plate** (All-rounder)
- Cost: 2500k
- Defense: High
- Damage: Medium
- Healing: Self-heal
- Best For: Dungeons, solo content

**Bow/Leather/Mage** (Ranged DPS)
- Cost: 1800k
- Defense: Medium
- Damage: High
- Mobility: Fast
- Best For: PvE, farming

**Mace/Plate/Holy** (Tank + Healer)
- Cost: 2700k
- Defense: Very High
- Healing: Full support
- Best For: Group dungeons, guilds
