# Albion Online: Economy & Market

## Market Mechanics

### Trading System
- **Auction House**: Buy/sell items, takes 0.5% tax
- **Guild Market**: In-guild trading, no tax
- **Direct Trade**: Player-to-player, no record
- **Price Fluctuation**: Supply/demand based
- **Order Types**: Instant buy/sell or listed orders

### Market Servers
- **West Server**: Prime time US (peak 8pm-12am EST)
- **East Server**: Asian players, 12am-8am EST
- **Europe Server**: European prime time

## Flipping & Arbitrage

### Successful Flip Strategy
1. **Identify Opportunity**: Low-demand items with seasonal peaks
2. **Buy Signal**: Price drops 20% below average
3. **Accumulate**: Buy 100-1000 units
4. **Hold**: Wait 1-2 weeks for demand spike
5. **Sell**: Off-load at normal price (+30-50% profit)

### Best Items to Flip
- **Potions**: Demand spikes before wars
- **Food**: Limited supply, high markup
- **Enchanted Gear**: T6-T7 enchanted items
- **Seasonal Materials**: Winter/summer bonuses

### Flip Failure Risks
- **Price Crash**: Item demand disappears
- **Stuck Capital**: Money locked in unpopular item
- **Auction Tax**: 0.5% both ways (1% total)

## Market Trends & Cycles

### Weekly Patterns
- **Monday-Thursday**: Low prices, stable
- **Friday-Saturday**: Demand spike (wars), prices +30%
- **Sunday**: Cooling off, prices -10%

### Monthly Cycles
- **Start of Month**: Guild wars ramp, demand high
- **Mid-Month**: Stabilization, margins thin
- **End of Month**: Territory reset, chaos sells well

### Seasonal Trends
- **New Update**: All prices spike 40-50%
- **Patch Rebalance**: Counter-items become valuable
- **Event Periods**: Themed items +100%

## Silver Sinks (Where Money Goes)
- **Crafting Tax**: 5-10% of item value
- **Auction Tax**: 0.5% buy + 0.5% sell
- **Repair Costs**: 5-10% of item value per repair
- **Guild Taxes**: 5% of income for guild benefit

## Economic Strategy for New Players

1. **Week 1**: Farm dungeons, save 100k silver
2. **Week 2**: Start market flipping (low-risk items)
3. **Week 3**: Craft basic items, sell for profit
4. **Week 4**: Scale operation, hire crafters
5. **Goal**: 1m+ passive income per week by month 2
