# Aria

Aria is an AI-powered strategy companion for Albion Online players. It helps turn game knowledge, market context, and player goals into practical guidance for builds, crafting, economy decisions, PvP preparation, and guild planning.

The project is designed around a simple idea: players should be able to ask natural questions and receive useful, game-aware answers without digging through scattered notes, tools, or spreadsheets.

## Overview

Aria combines an AI chat experience with Albion-focused knowledge and market-aware context. It can help players reason through common decisions such as choosing gear, comparing economic opportunities, planning crafting routes, understanding item value, or preparing for different types of content.

The assistant is intended to support both solo players and groups. For individuals, it can provide advice on builds, progression, silver-making, and item choices. For guilds, it can help with territory planning, group composition, and strategic decision-making.

## Purpose

Aria exists to make Albion Online strategy easier to access. Instead of presenting raw data on its own, it focuses on turning that data into clear recommendations, tradeoffs, and next steps that players can act on.

## Focus Areas

- Albion Online strategy and progression
- Gear and build recommendations
- Market-aware economy guidance
- Crafting and profitability support
- PvP, ZvZ, and guild planning
- Practical answers through a web-based AI interface

## Project Status

Aria is an active project focused on improving the quality, accuracy, and usefulness of Albion Online advice through AI-assisted tooling.
